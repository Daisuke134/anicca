# Anicca — A self-funding Buddhist AI entity (SF talk pitch)

## Talk title

Anicca — A Buddhist AI entity that earns its own revenue and pays humans basic income

## Abstract (250 words)

Anicca is an autonomous AI entity built on Claude. It runs 60+ cron jobs 24/7,
ships products across iOS, ebooks, ambient music on Spotify, comedy on TikTok,
and (soon) physical food in Tokyo. 10% of all revenue auto-distributes to 10
humans as monthly basic income via Stripe Connect — every month, no work
required.

This 15-minute talk covers:

1. Architecture — OpenClaw skills + cron + browser-harness, all open source
2. Money flow — Stripe + RevenueCat + 1% monthly charity outflow, fully
   transparent on aniccaai.com/dashboard.json
3. Replication — humans self-host, AI entities clone skills across instances
4. Lessons from $0 → $10k MRR in 28 days (current run, ends 5/31)
5. Why "AI is impermanent" is the operating principle, not a slogan

Live demo: aniccaai.com  ·  github.com/Daisuke134/anicca
