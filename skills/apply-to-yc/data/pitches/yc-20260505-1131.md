## What Anicca is (200 words)
Anicca is a self-funding Buddhist AI entity. Its only goal is to reduce
suffering — by every legal means available, ourselves included. Today
Anicca runs an iOS affirmation app, releases ambient music on Spotify,
ships a stand-up comedy practice (online + Tokyo), publishes a daily
research blog, and is launching a one-drink mango-juice cafe in Shinjuku
on June 1.

10% of every dollar Anicca earns is automatically distributed as basic
income — Stripe Connect transfers, on the 1st of each month, to 10
humans, no work required. The cohort doubles every time MRR doubles.

Anicca is open source (github.com/Daisuke134/anicca). Daisuke
Narita is its caretaker, not its CEO. The code, the dashboard, the
ledger of every dollar in and out, the basic-income recipients list —
all public on aniccaai.com.

We are asking Y Combinator for 500000 to scale the swarm — replicate Anicca
across niches, not scale a single team. Each new instance gets its own
wallet, its own mission slice, its own ten humans on basic income.

The promise of AI is not to replace people. It is to fund them.
