# Prompt Pack

## Hook extraction
Analyze this TikTok slideshow and identify:
- the main hook in slide 1
- why it works
- the structure of the hook
- 5 English-only hook variants
- 5 Pinterest search queries for matching visuals

## Slide generation
Create a 6-slide English-only TikTok slideshow with:
- the original hook preserved
- one idea per slide
- minimal text on each slide
- a consistent visual style
- a soft final slide

## Caption
Write a short English caption with:
- 1 sentence hook recap
- 1 sentence value promise
- 3 to 6 relevant hashtags
- no hard CTA unless requested

## Scheduling note
Schedule for the target audience's local evening peak, not the creator's timezone unless they match.
