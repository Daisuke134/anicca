# Posting Time

## Default rule
For English-only audiences, schedule in the audience's local time zone during evening peak.

## Default windows
- General consumer content: 7:00 pm to 10:00 pm local time
- Utility or educational content: 6:30 pm to 9:00 pm local time
- Curiosity-first content: 8:00 pm to 11:00 pm local time

## If audience geography is unknown
Use US evening time.
- Best default: 8:30 pm US local time
- Practical fallback: 9:00 pm US local time

## Decision rule
- If the post is broad and English-only, use 8:30 pm local time.
- If the post is tied to a known country, convert 8:30 pm in that country.
- If there are multiple English-speaking regions, use the largest audience region.

## Do not
- Do not schedule at random daytime hours.
- Do not schedule in the creator's time zone unless it matches the target audience.
- Do not use direct publish when draft review is available.
