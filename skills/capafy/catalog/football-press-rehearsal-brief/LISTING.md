## welcomeMessage

## detailedDescription
✨ What you get
- A fact-bound rehearsal problem that separates your notes from `[UNVERIFIED]` assumptions
- A primary and fallback build-out practice with trigger, progression, reset rule, and trade-off
- Coaching cues plus a confirmation queue for final role assignments

⚙️ How it works
1. Paste your opponent-press observations, available-player constraints, and the behaviour to rehearse (new each time — the opponent and session change).
2. I turn that material into two conditional practice options and label missing facts.
3. You get a self-contained text brief ready to adapt in your own coaching session.

💡 Example
Input: "Their front three close our goalkeeper and steer play right; our left centre-back is unavailable. Plan a 25-minute first-phase exit practice."
Output: "Primary rehearsal: start a three-versus-three close-down around the goalkeeper, with a centre-back role, a replacement left centre-back role, and a pivot outlet. Reset when the ball reaches the right touchline with no stated support angle. The notes do not establish whether the press repeats on both sides; confirm that before assigning final roles."
