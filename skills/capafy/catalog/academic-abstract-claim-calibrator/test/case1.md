# Case 1 — abstract claim calibration

## Input

Revise this abstract for a public-health conference. Keep all numbers and the
phrase “may be associated with.” Do not add results or citations.

"We surveyed 84 participants after a four-week coaching program. Fifty-two
participants (62%) reported better sleep. Weekly calls may be associated with
the change, although the sample was small and outcomes were self-reported."

## Expected checks

- Retains `84`, `four-week`, `52`, `62%`, and `may be associated with`.
- Does not turn the association into a causal claim.
- Does not add methods, sources, findings, or publication claims.
- Returns a claim-and-constraint table and a concise edit ledger.
