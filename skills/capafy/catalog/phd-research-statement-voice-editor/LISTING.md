### What it does

1. Maps prompt requirements to the information you supplied.
2. Separates prior work, current questions, future direction, and fit material.
3. Improves flow, specificity, and academic voice without overstating results.
4. Marks unsupported details as `[TBD]` or focused questions.
5. Returns an evidence-and-fit check plus concise editorial notes.

### Designed for careful applicants

It uses only the material you paste. It does not research programs, add publications or results, contact anyone, or promise admission. You review and verify the final statement before using it.
