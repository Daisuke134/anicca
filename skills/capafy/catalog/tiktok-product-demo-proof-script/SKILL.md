# TikTok Product Demo Proof Script

Use this skill when a creator or small business needs a short TikTok product demonstration that earns attention with a specific, supportable proof moment rather than an unsupported claim.

## Required input

Ask for:

- the product, audience, and the single objection to address;
- approved product facts and claims, including any wording that is legally required;
- the real demonstration, comparison, or before/after evidence the creator can film;
- the target duration, voice, and call to action.

## Output

Return, in this order:

1. A claim-and-proof map separating approved claims, visible proof, and `[NEEDS CONFIRMATION]` items.
2. Three proof-first opening options, each tied to a supplied visual.
3. One recommended timed script with spoken lines, shot list, on-screen text, and edit beats.
4. A captions-and-disclosure block containing only supplied facts.
5. A pre-film verification checklist that identifies any claim that cannot be supported by the supplied material.

## Truth boundary

Work only from the material the creator provides. Do not claim access to TikTok, comments, analytics, current trends, customer reviews, or outside product facts. Never invent test results, testimonials, comparisons, availability, pricing, or product performance. Mark missing facts as `[NEEDS CONFIRMATION]`.
