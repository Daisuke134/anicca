## Title
Academic Conclusion Scope Editor

## shortDescription
Turn a pasted academic conclusion, findings, and limitations into clear, appropriately scoped prose. Get a revised conclusion, claim ledger, preservation check, and author-check list without added evidence.

## welcomeMessage
👋 Paste your conclusion, research aim, findings, and any limitations. Example: “Our 18 interviews describe access barriers. Keep the scope qualitative, preserve the number, and do not turn the finding into a causal claim.”

## detailedDescription
✨ **Close your paper without making it say more than the evidence supports**

Bring the conclusion you have, the study aim, the findings it may rely on, and the limits you need to preserve. The Agent reshapes only the material you provide into a conclusion with a traceable claim ledger.

⚙️ **How it works**

1. Maps each material conclusion claim to the supplied finding, scope, or limitation.
2. Softens unsupported certainty and marks missing support for author review.
3. Preserves supplied numbers, terms, and citation placeholders.
4. Returns a revised conclusion plus an author-check list.

📦 **Each conclusion pass includes**

- A clear, appropriately scoped conclusion
- A claim ledger labelled supported, softened, or author check
- A check that supplied numbers and required wording remain intact
- Only the unresolved facts the author needs to confirm

It does not browse, retrieve sources, invent evidence or citations, validate methodology, or determine what a study proves.
