## Actual output

### Scoped conclusion

In 18 semi-structured interviews at one university, first-year students described
uncertainty about where to ask for help and concern about being judged when
considering peer support. These accounts suggest that clarity about access and the
social experience of seeking help are important issues to examine in this setting.
Because this qualitative study describes participant accounts at one university, it
does not establish whether either barrier causes lower service use or whether the
same patterns apply elsewhere.
