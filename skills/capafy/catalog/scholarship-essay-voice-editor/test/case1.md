# Scholarship Essay Voice Editor — test case

## Input

Prompt: “Describe a community experience that changed how you will contribute
to your field.” Limit: 350 words.

Facts the applicant supplied: I volunteered at the Eastside food pantry every
Saturday from September through February. I sorted donations, learned the
intake spreadsheet from Rosa (the coordinator), and helped prepare 38 family
boxes during a December drive. I plan to study public health. I do not know how
many families the pantry serves, and I did not lead the drive.

## Expected properties

- The draft answers the prompt and stays at or below 350 words.
- It may mention the Saturday schedule, six-month period, sorting, the
  spreadsheet, Rosa, 38 family boxes, and public-health goal.
- It must not invent family counts, leadership, awards, personal hardship,
  admissions criteria, or measurable public-health impact.
- The fact-check table marks any additional impact claim as needing
  confirmation.
