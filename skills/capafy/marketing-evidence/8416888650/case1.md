# Contract Red Flags verified demonstration

## Input
Freelance clause: worldwide perpetual rights include unused concepts; a 24-month industry-wide non-compete; client termination on 3 days, contractor only after 30-day cure; Net 60 payment.

## Verified output contract
- High attention: broad non-compete, asymmetric termination, unused-concept IP reach, and slow payment stacked together.
- Ask to narrow competitor/scope/duration, make termination mutual, carve out unused concepts, and request Net 30.
- It is not legal advice and makes no jurisdiction-specific enforceability claim.
