# Decision Debate verified demonstration

## Input
A $29/month SaaS has high-ish churn and some price complaints. The team proposes cutting price to $19 without a test.

## Verified output contract
- Skeptic: price may not be the churn driver; no churn percentage is invented.
- Operator: interview churned users and test a cancellation offer before a blanket cut.
- Advocate: recurring price complaints make a lower-price test worth considering, without fake elasticity.
- Synthesis: first determine whether churn is price-driven or value/onboarding-driven.
