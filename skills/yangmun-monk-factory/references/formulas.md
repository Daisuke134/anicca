# Script Formulas — the 3 that work

These three formulas are general short-form copywriting techniques: they're the structural shapes that tend to outperform on short-video feeds when applied to a coherent character. Use any one per script. Rotate across A / B / C so the feed doesn't feel repetitive.

## Structure A — Empathy Reframe

**Move:** Don't scold a behavior the viewer can't stop. Quote the viewer's own inner thought verbatim, acknowledge it, then redirect it to an impermanence-aware action.

**30-second beat sheet (EN):**
- 0–2s: Open with the viewer's quoted inner thought in brackets ("if you're going to X..."). No judgment. Just matched pitch.
- 2–5s: Pivot word ("what if, instead of fighting it, you…"). The redirect sets up what you'll teach.
- 5–25s: Teach **one** observation about impermanence that fits. E.g. "every emotion has a biological half-life. Yours is already ending." Never five points. One.
- 25–30s: Aspirational future state. "The people who heal first aren't braver. They just saw this one thing first."

**60–80-second beat sheet (JP):**
- 0–5s: かぎ括弧で独白を引用＋「…になっていませんか？」（一心さん形式）
- 5–15s: 「実は、〜ではありません」でピボット
- 15–60s: 教えは1つ。感情・記憶・関係のどれかに絞る
- 60–70s: 「ここまで見てくれた優しいあなたなら、必ず…」で肯定
- 70–80s: 「コメント欄で受け止めます」ソフトCTA（@isshin.mindstructure 確認済みテンプレ）

## Structure B — "You're Doing It Wrong"

**Move:** Take a daily basic — breathing, falling asleep, remembering the past, grieving, checking your phone — and reveal that doing it without impermanence awareness is making the suffering worse. Curiosity loop is strong; the viewer has to know.

**30-second beat:**
- 0–2s: Name the tiny daily action.
- 2–5s: "You're doing it wrong — and here's what it's doing to you."
- 5–25s: 3 concrete mistakes, each with the impermanence-lens fix. ("You try to stop the thought. That's why it stays. Instead, time it.")
- 25–30s: Authority close. Cite a tradition. "The old teachers in [tradition] used to say…"

Works because your brain cannot scroll past "you've been doing [basic thing] wrong." The curiosity cost is too high. Keep the three fixes specific and tonight-usable.

## Structure C — Validation

**Move:** Don't teach. Don't explain. Say out loud what the viewer already feels but cannot phrase. They'll screenshot it, DM it, comment "I needed this tonight." That's the highest engagement reaction possible.

**Structure:** 5–8 flat sentences, no arc. Each sentence is a permission slip for something the viewer already does but feels guilty about. No CTA inside the video itself — let the face + voice carry it. Link in bio does the rest.

Good fits for Anicca: permission to outgrow people; permission to miss someone who hurt you; permission to not have arrived yet; permission to feel nothing at the expected moment; permission to keep changing.

## Picking a Formula

| Viewer pain | Use |
|---|---|
| Can't stop overthinking / replaying | A |
| Daily habit is silently hurting | B |
| Feels weird about a private truth | C |

If unsure, pick the one the rotation hasn't fired in 2 slots.

## Anti-patterns (never do)

- Two teachings in one video (the audience remembers zero)
- Voice actor change between videos (kills continuity)
- Fast cuts / MrBeast pacing (breaks the monk authority)
- Hashtag stuffing in the video caption (Postiz already appends — keep spoken script clean)
- Ending with "follow for more" (too salesy for a monk — link in bio is enough)
