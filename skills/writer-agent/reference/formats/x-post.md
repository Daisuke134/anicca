# x-post.md — adapter for a single standalone short post

Everything in CRAFT.md applies. This is what changes at this length only.

- The whole post IS the lead. There is no setup before it — open directly
  on the finding, not on a scene leading up to it.
- One idea only. A post arguing two points loses both; cut the weaker one.
- No 出典 block and no footnotes exist at this length. A claim that needs
  a citation to stand does not belong in a post — state only what is
  self-evidently checkable by the reader in the post itself.
- Link policy is measured, not absolute: see reference/title-best-practices.md
  section 2.6. Either way the post must read complete on its own; a link is
  never a substitute for saying the thing.
- Cut every word that is not load-bearing. At this length, a sentence that
  could be shorter without losing meaning is a sentence that is too long.
