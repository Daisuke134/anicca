# longform.md — adapter for a book chapter

Everything in CRAFT.md applies. This is what changes at this length only.

- A chapter needs its own local lead and payoff even while it serves the
  book's larger throughline — a reader who opens to this chapter alone
  still needs a reason to keep reading it.
- Terms and concepts may be defined once, early in the book, and referred
  back to by name in later chapters without redefinition — the "stands
  alone" rule relaxes across chapters of the same book, because a book is
  read in order, unlike a single article or post.
- Callbacks across chapters must still restate the POINT in one fresh
  clause, not just the term — a bare "as Chapter 2 showed" is still banned.
- Citations may accumulate into one book-level bibliography rather than
  per-chapter, but a claim inside the chapter still needs its Claim |
  Evidence | Status at the point it's made.
- Each chapter closes on a line that earns the turn to the next chapter,
  not a summary that restates the chapter just read.
