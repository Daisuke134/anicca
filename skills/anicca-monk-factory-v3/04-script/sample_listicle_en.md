HOOK:
Five signs your body is carrying a tiredness that sleep cannot fix, my friend.

RETENTION:
Stay with me until the end — the last sign is the one almost no one notices.

AUTHORITY:
I am Ajahn Sutta. For fifty years I have watched the body and the mind, in silence.

BODY:
The first sign — you wake at three in the morning, and the mind begins to run.
This is not broken sleep. It is a body that never felt safe enough to set the day down.

The second — your shoulders stay heavy, even after rest.
You are carrying tomorrow on your back before it has arrived.

The third — in the dark, your hand reaches for the phone.
The silence feels too loud, so you fill it. But the noise only feeds the fire.

The fourth — small things bring you near tears, and you cannot say why.
The body is asking to put down a weight you have carried far too long.

The fifth, and the deepest — you feel tired of resting itself.
When that happens, the tiredness is no longer in the body. It is in the holding on.

TURN:
So tonight, do not try to mend all of it. Breathe out, once, slowly.
Let one thing go — the way the river lets the leaf pass.

CTA:
If this found you, comment ENOUGH below.
Share it with someone who has been tired for too long.
And follow me, my friend. …and that is enough.
