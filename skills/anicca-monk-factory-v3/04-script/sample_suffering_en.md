If you keep replaying what they did to you, my friend — at red lights, in the
shower, at three in the morning — listen carefully. This is not a weak mind.
And tonight I will give you the exact way to set it down. Stay until the end,
for the last question is the one that frees you.

I am Ajahn Sutta. For fifty years I have watched the mind, in silence.

First, understand this. You believe you hold the anger to protect yourself.
But you are not holding it, my friend. It is holding you. Every time you replay
it, they harm you again — but now you are the one pressing play.

So tonight, before sleep, do this. It takes three breaths and three questions.

Bring their face to mind — gently, do not run from it.
Then ask, slowly: First — what were they carrying that I could not see?
Second — what is this anger costing me, tonight, in my own chest, my own sleep?
Third — if I set it down for just one hour, only one, what would I feel?

You do not have to forgive them. That is not the task tonight.
You only have to stop carrying them into your bed. Set them by the door.
They will still be there in the morning, if you truly want them back.

This is the practice. The river does not keep the leaf. It lets it pass.

If you will try the three questions tonight, comment SET IT DOWN below.
Share this with someone who is still carrying something heavy.
And follow me, my friend. …and that is enough.
