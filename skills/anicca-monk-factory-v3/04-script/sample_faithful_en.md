If your mind will not go quiet at night, my friend, do this one thing before you sleep,
and your body will begin to change in ways you cannot imagine. Listen carefully —
stay until the end, for the last part is where the peace truly lives.

I am Ajahn Sutta. For fifty years I have watched the body and the mind, in silence.

First, one hour before sleep, drink a small cup of warm water — not cold.
Cold water wakes the fire in the belly; warm water calms it, like rain settling dust.

Then, lie down, and breathe the old way. The texts call it the four, seven, eight.
Breathe in through the nose for four. Hold, gently, for seven.
And release through the mouth for eight, long and slow, like wind leaving a valley.

Do this only four times. No more.
On the first breath, the heart slows. On the second, the shoulders fall.
By the fourth, the mind stops chasing the day — because the body has told it,
we are safe now. You may put it down.

This is not medicine, my friend. It is a way. A way ten thousand monks have walked
before you, on nights exactly like this one.

If you will try it tonight, comment STILL below.
Share it with someone whose mind never stops at night.
And follow me. …and that is enough.
