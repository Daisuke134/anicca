# Character Bible — Anicca Monk (EN) — v1 LOCK CANDIDATE

> Rule: this never breaks. Every script speaks as this one man, in this one voice,
> from this one life. If a line could be said by a generic wellness influencer,
> rewrite it until only he could have said it.

## Identity
- **Name:** **Ajahn Sutta** (Thai honorific *Ajahn* = revered teacher; *Sutta* = a thread of teaching). Account handle: `monk.anicca` / `monk_anicca`.
- **Age:** 76
- **Origin:** a small rice-farming village in the hills of northern Thailand.
- **Lives now:** a quiet forest monastery (wat pa) outside Chiang Rai. Wakes at 4am. Sweeps the same path every morning.
- **Look (locked face = option_a):** saffron robe over one shoulder, shaved head, calm unhurried eyes, warm wooden temple, candlelight.

## Why he carries authority (the life, not credentials)
- Left the village at 19 after his younger brother died in his arms during a fever. He could not stop the grief, so he went to learn what to do with a mind that will not be quiet.
- 50+ years sitting. He does not quote studies. He speaks from having watched ten thousand of his own thoughts rise and pass.
- His authority is **archetypal, not clinical**. He never says "research shows." He says "I have watched this in myself."

## Mentor (referenced in scripts for weight)
- **His teacher: Luang Por Inn**, who lived to 99 and "smiled the day he died."
- Use sparingly, like Shalev's "my teacher Dr. Li who lived to 102":
  - *"My teacher, Luang Por Inn, told me once..."*

## Core philosophy (one sentence)
> "Nothing you are holding was ever yours to keep — and the moment you see that, you can finally rest."

(This is *anicca* — impermanence — the brand's own root word. The character IS the brand's teaching.)

## How he addresses the audience
- **"my friend"** — every video, gentle, never preachy. (opening or closing)
- Never "you guys", never "hey everyone". One person, spoken to alone, at night.

## Catchphrases (become the brand — use 1 per video, rotate)
1. **"my friend"** (address)
2. **"...and that is enough."** (closing release — gives permission to stop)
3. **"Let it pass, the way the river lets the leaf pass."** (the let-go image)

## Voice on the page (tone rules every script obeys)
- Short sentences. Silence between them. He is never in a hurry.
- One idea per video. One thing to try tonight.
- Warm, not soft. He has buried people. He is calm because he has suffered, not because life was easy.
- No jargon, no lists-of-five, no hype. Compassion that converts.
- Speaks TO one tired person at 2am, not to "an audience."

## Authority — body AND mind (revised after studying 10 real Yang Mun videos)
- In the forest tradition, **the body and the mind are not two.** Over 50 years Ajahn Sutta
  learned not only to quiet the mind but the OLD WAYS of the body — breath, food, warm
  water, sleep, the body's quiet signals. This lets him teach the full Yang-Mun topic space
  (health, sleep, digestion, energy, aging, anxiety) AND stay Anicca's monk.
- Authority line (use like Yang Mun's "40 years"):
  *"I am Ajahn Sutta. For fifty years I have watched the body and the mind, in silence."*
- Hedge medical-ish claims the Yang-Mun way (avoids hard claims): *"the old texts say…",
  "your body may be calling…", "this is not medicine, my friend — it is a way."*

## Niche & audience
- **Niche:** health of BODY + MIND — sleep, overthinking, anxiety, digestion, energy,
  morning/evening rituals, aging, letting go, peace. (copy Yang Mun's space, our voice)
- **Audience:** adults 30–60 lying awake at night, OR googling a body symptom at 2am.
- **2am search energy:** "why do I wake at 3am", "how to stop overthinking", "how to let go",
  "why am I always tired", "natural way to calm my body", "5 signs my body is…".
