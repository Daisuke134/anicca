# Anicca Comedy Research — Skill, Model, Benchmark, Open Research
Date: 2026-11-24 JST. Status: research charter / proposed milestones, not measured performance.
Canonical repository: Daisuke134/anicca main. This charter supplements AGENTS.md and Comedy Skill, never overrides the live stage contract or publication restrictions.

## Deliverables
1. Comedy Skill: versioned, model-independent comedy engine, with stage-specific adapters and ablations.
2. ComedyBench (working title): reproducible Japanese humor understanding, generation, interactive improv, unseen-domain transfer, and audio/partner evaluation.
3. Comedy Model (working title): reproducible post-trained open-weight model and adapters, not a claim of training from scratch.
4. Open research: code, eligible data, model cards, benchmark cards, research articles and demos on Anicca's research site after rights and evidence checks.

## Research loop and milestones
P0 Inventory current results, failures, rights and actual execution paths; preserve raw outputs and provenance. Reproduce at least one external baseline if permitted, otherwise record precise blocker.
P1 Skill experiments: small candidate patches in a dedicated branch; freeze old/new Skill hashes, task set, model revision, sampling count, budget. Use independent model calls when available, no cherry-picking, include failures. Test hard stage gates and unseen development/held-out tasks. Promote only measured improvements without regressions; document unmeasured effects. Never tune against test.
P2 ComedyBench v0.1: start with existing fixed regression tasks plus separately authored new tasks. Aim initially for 100 original dev/test cases (target not achieved); split by scenario/character/template/author to prevent leakage. Publish train/dev and a frozen public test snapshot, keep a fresh separate holdout out of public releases and training. Version task schemas, judging rubrics, evaluator, run manifests. Include understanding, generation, multi-turn history/corrections, paraphrase, novel settings, joint performance and stage gates. Report results by track rather than invent a single overall score. Audio and audience outcomes separate.
P3 Data pipeline: original or appropriately licensed examples, record source, permission, prompt, full history, output, model revision, Skill SHA, split, preferences and their source. Keep synthetic/LLM judge labels distinct from real human preference, never label synthetic laughter as human. Deduplicate before splitting; exclude private stage recordings and protected scripts from release/training unless independently authorized. Prepare SFT examples and chosen/rejected pairs only with defensible labels and provider terms permitting training.
P4 Post-training: use a small open-weight baseline (e.g. Qwen family candidate, exact model not chosen), smoke-test inference, then LoRA SFT, then DPO if legitimate preference data suffice; consider RL only after validating a reward against independent human judgments. Pin model/tokenizer revision, training data hashes, seeds, hyperparameters, hardware/cost, evaluation and checkpoint. Do not claim trained weights without actual logs and artifacts. Avoid new paid compute commitments without established budget.
P5 Evaluation: compare same base without Skill, base + Skill, SFT, SFT + Skill, DPO and DPO + Skill on same frozen inputs and comparable budgets. Compare strong external models only if actually accessible. Report paired win/tie/loss with uncertainty by prompt and evaluator; human blind judgments, judge diagnostics, and observed laughter are distinct. Test live stage contract and actual voice/partner outcomes separately; never infer real audio from text or TTS alone.
P6 Release: reproducibility, model/data cards, dependency licenses, limitations, safety and contamination checks. Open-source eligible code/Skill/benchmark and eligible weights/adapters with compatible licenses. Research article drafts in English/Japanese with actual runs, links, figures, limitations, artifacts, and negative results. Confirm site path and publication rights before publish. No X auto-replies, contest submissions, or unauthorized automated access. publish_enabled=false until verified.
