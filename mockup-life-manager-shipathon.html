The four answers

What are we building?

The first Life Manager feature removes one invisible burden: planning the trip between where you are and where you need to be.

WHAT APP?
Life Manager

A chat-first life assistant. You tell it a destination or an event, and it turns that into a plan you can actually follow.

Example: “I need to get to Shibuya Station by 14:00.”

FOR WHO?
People who have places to be

Students, workers, founders, travelers, and anyone who moves between appointments, stations, campuses, and events.

Example: Anyone who has been late because they forgot the trip itself.

WHAT PROBLEM?
Travel time creates invisible mental load

Calendar shows the event. Maps shows the route. You still have to connect them, calculate the buffer, remember when to leave, and notice the notification.

Example: The event starts at 14:00, but you only realize at 13:55 that the trip takes 35 minutes.

SOLVE HOW?
Turn the destination into a departure plan

Life Manager uses your current location and destination, calculates the route, explains every step in chat, and calls before it is time to leave.

Example: “Leave at 13:18. Walk to the station, take the recommended train, and arrive at Shibuya Station by 13:52.”

A real example

From where I am to Shibuya Station.

The user does not open Google Calendar, then Google Maps, then calculate the buffer. They simply tell Life Manager the destination. The plan comes back in the same chat.
