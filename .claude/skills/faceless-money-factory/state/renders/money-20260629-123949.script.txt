These are the exact four spending switches that helped me save $8,400 in six months without feeling deprived. Let's go.

First, implement a 24-hour rule for any nonessential purchase over $50. Set a timer and wait—this one change cut my impulse buys by 40 percent.

Next, use a zero-based budget where every dollar is assigned to a category before the month begins. This forces you to plan savings first, spending second, and keeps your balance growing at 20 percent of income.

The third, switch to a cash envelope system for variable expenses like dining out and entertainment. Pull out $200 in cash at the start of the month and when it's gone, it's gone.

And the last, automate a 15 percent direct deposit from each paycheck into a separate savings account before you ever see the money. This builds a buffer that covers three months of essential bills without any effort.
