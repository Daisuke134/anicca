These are the exact four budget categories that helped me cut my annual spending by 32% without feeling deprived. Let's go.

First, allocate only 50% of your after-tax income to fixed necessities like rent, utilities, and insurance. Second, cap your flexible wants at 30% of income this includes subscriptions, dining, and shopping. Third, the twist, assign 15% straight to retirement accounts before you touch any other savings. And the last, force 5% into a dedicated cash emergency fund as a hard rule, not a suggestion. That last 5% is what keeps you from ever dipping into credit cards when surprises hit.
