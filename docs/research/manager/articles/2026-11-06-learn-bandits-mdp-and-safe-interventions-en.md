# Can an AI Learn When Not to Intervene? The Science Behind Manager Phase 8
*November 6, 2026 | Research and educational draft only. Not published. No product efficacy claim.*

Predicting what a person will do is not the same as demonstrating that a notification will help them. Manager Phase 8 studies not only behavioral prediction but also the more demanding problem of choosing when to support a self-selected goal—and when to do nothing.

**Contextual bandits** learn which intervention may work in a given context. They balance exploration of uncertain options with exploitation of promising ones. However, exploration is not permission: driving, quiet hours, refusal, withdrawal, and other safety constraints must be enforced before any policy chooses an action. Existing implementations such as MABWiser provide LinUCB and Thompson Sampling methods; inventing a new bandit algorithm is not our first goal.

**Off-policy evaluation (OPE)** estimates a new policy's value from logged decisions. Inverse propensity scoring and doubly robust methods require correctly identified original action probabilities and adequate support. A corrected delivery indicator must never be mistaken for randomized assignment. Dudík et al. (2011): https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/publication/doubly-robust-policy-evaluation-and-learning-2/

**Markov decision processes (MDPs)** add future state changes to the objective. Today's prompt may improve immediate adherence yet worsen future fatigue or autonomy. **Partially observable MDPs (POMDPs)** recognize that motivation, distress, and habit strength are not directly observable: a belief state represents uncertainty, not a diagnosis or privileged access to someone's mind. A foundational reference is Sutton and Barto, Reinforcement Learning: An Introduction (2018): https://mitpress.mit.edu/9780262039246/reinforcement-learning/

Oralytics provides a valuable example of a real deployed online decision-making algorithm in oral-health research. Its 2025 description covers twice-daily decisions, a 70-day intervention, 79 enrolled participants, and analyses excluding seven participants affected by a data issue. This one-arm evidence must not be misrepresented as randomized proof of benefits against no intervention. A 2026 monitoring paper describes severity-classified incidents and prespecified fallbacks across Oralytics and MiWaves. Sources: https://pmc.ncbi.nlm.nih.gov/articles/PMC12122013/ ; https://www.nature.com/articles/s41746-026-02669-4

Manager's product architecture separates the predictive model, causal response estimates, policy, permissioned execution authority, and independent benefit/harm evaluation. Human studies require jurisdiction-specific protections, appropriate consent, and any necessary ethics review. In Japan, the August 2026 amendment to the biomedical human-subjects guidelines is scheduled to take effect on December 1, 2026, not yet in force on November 6: https://www.mhlw.go.jp/stf/newpage_75864.html

**Status:** P08-3 to P08-5 are taught and documented but not implemented or evaluated on people. Manager does not yet have a proven unified behavior foundation model or a human efficacy trial. The scientific standard is not notification count, engagement, or the apparent confidence of a model; it is independently measured benefit for user-defined goals, with uncertainty and harm fully reported.
