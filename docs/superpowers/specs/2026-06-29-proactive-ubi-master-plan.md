## 0. The non-negotiable principle (Dais 2026-06-29)

> "I really don't want this to be something where people have to sign into this thing and get it… It can be like that [at first] but the next phase is definitely not like that."

A **pull** model (people must find the website + sign up) **always produces exclusion**: people with no internet, no smartphone, no awareness, or no inclination are silently left out — the same way those who never heard of AI/cloud/Claude Code cannot benefit from it. For AGI to benefit **all** humanity (OpenAI's stated mission), UBI **must be PROACTIVE: it comes TO people, it does not wait for them to come to it**, and it **does not discriminate** (offline, illiterate, unbanked, unaware all included).

**There is no full proactive UBI in the world today.** Pieces exist (below). Our job is to assemble + extend them into the first one — and if a piece is "not possible yet," we take the action to **make** it possible (government + local-agency + carrier + NGO partnerships). And we **start distributing from this very moment**, however small, then grow.

The gap to close: **no one has fused (personhood-at-the-edge) + (push payout on existing rails) + (AI-funded, AI-run outreach) + (continuous growing stream) into a universal, opt-out, no-website UBI.** That fusion is this plan.

## 2. The 6-layer proactive architecture (target state)

1. **DISCOVERY** — AI finds humans, doesn't wait. Satellite + ML + population/census/mobile-network data maps where the *unreached* are. (GiveDirectly targeting, extended to all humanity.)
2. **REACH** — AI spends its own surplus to buy the means to arrive: personhood devices (Orb-type) deployed to markets/camps/villages, **mobile-carrier partnerships** (they already reach billions of feature phones), **field agent / NGO / government** enrollment, and increasingly **other AI agents (the colony)** running the outreach.
3. **DEFAULT-ENROLL (opt-out, not opt-in)** — offered on the spot to everyone in a community; the person takes **zero proactive action**; only those who decline are excluded.
4. **PERSONHOOD AT THE EDGE** — one-human-one-stream verified where the person is: in-person biometric (Orb/fingerprint) or phone-number + liveness; anonymous nullifier ledger blocks double-claims.
5. **PUSH PAYOUT on rails everyone already has** — mobile money to a SIM (USSD/SMS, no internet) + agent cash-out (M-Pesa model) / bank / USDC; delivered as a **continuous GDA stream** (1 tx → all members, every second, no human).
6. **RECURSIVE EXPANSION** — earnings + data from reached regions fund reaching the next unreached region; the system spends to find the **last unreached human**.

Core engine: **the AI's earnings fund the AI's own outreach** (GiveDirectly's 10% delivery cost, paid by AI surplus), and the outreach is increasingly **executed by AI agents**, so humans appear only as **recipients**.

## 3. Phased plan (start NOW, drive human-in-loop → 0)

### Phase 0 — START THIS MOMENT (opt-in allowed, but real money must flow now)
- Use what is already built: aniccaai.com/income (World ID personhood gate, LIVE), GDA pool (LIVE on Base), payout rails (Bridge/Crossmint/Kotani/GMO).
- **Goal**: a real UBI payout reaches a real verified human (USDC/mobile-money/bank) **now**, even at tiny scale. Proves the spine end-to-end.
- Acceptable that Phase 0 is opt-in (signup). It is the seed, not the destination.

### Phase 1 — Reduce friction (push to rails people already have)
- Shift from "visit a website" to "we reach your phone": SMS/USSD enrollment, phone-number + liveness personhood, mobile-money payout. Outreach to known contact lists / communities.

### Phase 2 — PROACTIVE via partnerships (the real shift)
- **Team up with**: national/local governments (benefit-transfer rails, ID), local agencies + NGOs (GiveDirectly-style village field enrollment), mobile carriers (feature-phone reach), Worldcoin Orb operator network (edge personhood).
- **Opt-out, not opt-in.** AI funds the outreach from surplus.
- For any "not possible yet" piece → an explicit action item to make it possible (MOU drafts, pilots, regulatory engagement).

### Phase 3 — AI-run, human-in-loop → 0
- The colony of AI agents runs discovery, partnership ops, device fleets; last-mile physical contact migrates from humans → deployed autonomous personhood devices → robots. Drive the residual human touch toward zero, funded by compounding AI earnings.

## 4. The amount GROWS over time (Dais)

> "as they become richer and richer the amount of money given to them is gonna be increasing more and more."

UBI is **not a fixed stipend**. The per-person stream rate is a function of total AI/colony surplus; as the AI economy compounds, **every recipient's stream increases monotonically**. Start small, grow forever. (Mechanically: GDA `flowRate` per member rises as surplus rises; never cut except by explicit safety stop.)

## 5. Honest limit (asymptote, not a compromise)

Fully zero physical-contact reach to a person with no phone in a remote area still needs *some* physical presence today (humans now → devices → robots). We **do not** claim instant universal zero-touch. We **do** refuse the pull/website-only model from the start and drive the last-mile human-in-loop toward zero with AI earnings.
