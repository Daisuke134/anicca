## P1 — Product Hunt (English)

**Name:** Life Manager

**Tagline (50 chars):**
```
Hand off your calendar. Show up early, every time.
```

**Description (APPROVED — last "$20/mo / by phone and email / English or Japanese" line deleted per Dais):**
```
You're late again. People notice. Each time, they trust you a little less.

Life Manager fixes the behavior. It holds your calendar so you don't have to, calls your phone before each thing, names the place and the route, and talks you out the door. You arrive early, not just on time. It blocks your travel time so the commute is always accounted for, asks you by email when it doesn't know a place, and warns the people waiting when you run behind, after you approve the message.

Give it a week. You stop being the one people wait on. You're early to everything, and they start counting on you again.
```

**Maker's first comment (Dais's true pain):**
```
I used to be the guy who's always ten minutes late. Not by much, but enough that people stopped counting on me. Reminders didn't fix it. I'd swipe them away. So I built Life Manager. It holds my calendar, calls me before each thing, names my route, and talks me out the door. Now I show up early, and people count on me again. The point isn't the calls. It's that you stop being late, and that changes how people see you. Connect your Google Calendar and add your number to try it.
```
