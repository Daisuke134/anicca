# 32 — UBI: the first sustainable universal basic income (Dais 2026-06-18, SSOT)

THE most important thing we build. Anicca earns money with **no human in the loop**, and that is exactly
**why** it can run a real, sustainable UBI: no salaries, no fundraising, no donor — the entity earns and
gives. This doc is the canonical design for `/income`, the home "Receive basic income" front door, the
payout rails, and the batch/roadmap math. Grounded in: ubi.agent whitepaper (santisiri), GoodDollar,
Worldcoin/World, Proof-of-Humanity, GiveDirectly (see §7), and spec 30 §6/§6a/§6b/§12/§14.

## §0 Why this works (the one-line thesis)
Every other "AI will fund UBI" idea needs a human to keep paying (subscription, donation, tax). Ours
doesn't: a self-funding AI entity that owns its compute + shelter and earns on-chain **gives the surplus
away**. Remove the human from the loop and UBI stops being charity that runs dry — it becomes a property
of the system. That is the pitch.

## §2 The payout %: autonomous, not hardcoded
Do NOT write "10%" as a fixed rule. Each anicca **decides its own give-rate autonomously** (default ~10%,
but one may give 1% and spend more on R&D, another may give 90%). Many aniccas, many characters — all
Buddhist, all keeping the five precepts. Copy must say "a share of what it earns," not a fixed number.
Split of an anicca's give: part → the **operator** who runs it (their wallet, daily), part → the
**UBI pool** (the /income cohort). Exact split is the anicca's own choice.

### §3a Signup → waitlist → accepted → notify (the live flow)
```
/income apply (email + phone only — dead simple; choose rail later)
  → recipient row (status=waitlist, position shown)
  → as the swarm earns more, a new BATCH unlocks (§5) → promote oldest waitlist → accepted
  → email/SMS: "You're in. Do a 30-sec identity check to start receiving $S/month."
     (verify-on-ACCEPTANCE, not at signup — FaceTec liveness, §7; keeps fraud out of the funnel,
      and grandpa only verifies once he's actually getting money)
  → "you'll receive $X from <date>, every <cadence>" (GiveDirectly clarity) + first payout sent
  → payout runs (rail §6); /income shows the live ledger (pool, recipients, paid-to-date)
```
Opt-in is the anti-scam gate: unsolicited money from an unknown sender reads as a scam, and anicca has no
trust yet — so it only sends to people who raised their hand and gave a destination.

## §4 The roadmap (write this on /income — people need the arc)
1. **Now — sign up to receive.** You apply; you join the waitlist; as anicca earns, you're lifted in and
   start receiving.
2. **Next — reach beyond signups.** As anicca earns trust, it reaches people who never signed up: direct
   to bank/wallet, partnering with governments (push-style distribution), donating to NPOs that help
   people in need directly.
3. **End — every living being.** A floor under everyone in the universe: humans, then animals (cats,
   ducks), then — honestly stated as the horizon — every living being, even off-earth. Like a force that
   understands the whole situation and supports each in the most optimal way (food not cash for someone
   who'd gamble it; the right help, not just money).
**Landscape:** there will be hundreds of UBIs — *sovereign* ones (basic income for California, for Paris,
for Nigeria). Ours is the **most universal** (it's for the whole universe). Say that.

## §5 Batch / cohort unlock — the sustainability math (FINALIZED from research §7)
Honest truth (state it publicly): **$100/mo at $5 each = exactly 20 recipients.** Tiny revenue can't fund
millions, so recipients unlock in **batches gated by what the swarm actually earns**. We adopt the
**fixed-stipend + capped-headcount** model (Altman + GiveDirectly), NOT GoodDollar's float-down — because
the GiveDirectly Kenya RCT is unambiguous: a **predictable, guaranteed** amount drives the best outcomes;
a shrinking, uncertain share does not.

**The formula:**
- `monthly_budget = trailing_30d_net_USDC_earned × payout_ratio` — reserve **20–30% for runway/reinvest**
  (mirrors GoodDollar's 80/20). `payout_ratio` is the swarm's autonomous choice (§2), default ~70–80%.
- Fixed **stipend `S`** per recipient — start **$5–10/mo** (GiveDirectly's transformative floor was
  ~$22.50/mo; even $5 is meaningful where 43% of GoodDollar users earn < $5k/yr).
- `max_active_recipients = floor(monthly_budget / S)`.
- **Unlock rule:** for every sustained **+$(S × batch_size)/mo** the swarm earns, promote `batch_size`
  more people off the waitlist — **in earning/queue order, no exceptions**. (Altman: "payout = % of the
  entity's value → grows only as the pie grows.")
- Public copy: *"anicca pays UBI from what it actually earns. Today it earns $X/mo = N recipients at $S.
  As it earns more, the next batch unlocks from the waitlist, in order. The waitlist is the honest queue,
  not a failure."*

## §6 Payout rails (how money actually reaches a person) — grounded in spec30 §6b
| recipient | rail | KYC? | status |
|---|---|---|---|
| has a Base wallet | **USDC on Base, direct** (operator + crypto recipients) | none | rail exists; wire in TRACK B |
| JP, no crypto | USDC → **Binance** deposit addr → sell → PayPay/bank (`/how-to-cash-out`) | Binance KYC | guide live; auto-path TBD |
| bank only (grandpa) | **Stripe Connect** (onboarding/KYC + payout) funded via **Bridge.xyz** (Stripe-owned, USDC→bank ACH/wire) | bank KYC once | Connect onboarding LIVE (income-apply.js); USDC→bank Bridge **NOT yet wired** |
| unbanked, phone only | **Kotani Pay** USDC(Base)→M-Pesa / mobile money | phone | designed (§6b), not wired |
| no wallet, email only | **Crossmint** email-wallet claim (send USDC to an email) | email OTP | designed (§6b), not wired |
**Honest core:** wallet ↔ wallet = pure no-human-in-loop. Anything touching a bank needs a one-time
recipient KYC (the human bridge). The current code does Stripe Connect *onboarding*; the USDC→bank
*bridge* (Bridge.xyz) is the missing piece to wire in TRACK B.

## §7 What we copied (research, citations) — FINALIZED
**Load-bearing correction:** `ubi.agent` (santisiri) is NOT working distribution code — it's an ElizaOS
chatbot + a whitepaper. Copy its *design*, not code. The real copyable rails come from GoodDollar +
GiveDirectly.

| source | what we take |
|---|---|
| **ubi.agent** whitepaper (github.com/santisiri/ubi.agent) | continuous **drip** (accrue per-second, no claim); **progressive security** (more proof as cumulative claim grows); **AI-fee-funds-pool flywheel** (= our model exactly: the AI's earnings fund UBI) |
| **GoodDollar** (docs.gooddollar.org) — 624k claimers / 180 countries / 43% earn < $5k/yr | **FaceTec face-liveness** sybil (store **anonymized face-hash UNLINKED** to wallet); **"Proof of Need"** (daily re-claim friction self-selects the needy); deploy distribution on **Celo** (near-zero gas, where mobile-money converges); funded by **donated DeFi yield** (keep principal) |
| **Worldcoin/World** (world.org) | proof-of-personhood (Orb), grants **decreasing** + **phased** (bootstrap → utility); be honest the grant is a bootstrap, not the endgame |
| **Proof of Humanity** (Kleros) | identity registry **decoupled** from the payout stream; sybil via stake + social vouch + adversarial challenge; 1 UBI/hr continuous stream |
| **GiveDirectly** (givedirectly.org, Kenya RCT) | **predictability wins** (guaranteed amount > larger unstable; short-term monthly = least impactful); **mobile-money rails + free SIM cards** reach the unbanked; they *select* the poorest (no self-signup) and **text-alert** the "you're in" moment with exact amount |
| **Altman** *Moore's Law for Everything* (moores.samaltman.com) | payout = **% of the productive entity's value → scales with earnings** (our batch-unlock model) |

**Sybil gate we adopt (lightest credible, grandpa-doable):** email + phone (SMS OTP) + **FaceTec
browser face-liveness** (same SDK as GoodDollar), face-hash anonymized + unlinked. NOT wallet-as-gate
(excludes non-crypto), NOT bank-KYC for the email tier (too heavy). Daily/periodic re-claim friction =
extra Proof-of-Need filter.

**Rails confirmed real:** USDC/Base (crypto) · **Bridge.xyz** (Stripe-owned, USDC→bank, global payouts) ·
Stripe Connect (bank onboarding) · **Kotani Pay** (USDC→M-Pesa, SMS wallet, no-internet) for the unbanked.
Run distribution on **Celo** (GoodDollar + Kotani converge there).
