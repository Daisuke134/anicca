## 2. Booking rule (THE most important operational habit)

- ★ **Book on the 1st of every month, for the NEXT month's trip.** ★ (e.g. Jul 1 → book the August trip.)
- **Ideal = 3 months in advance. Minimum = 1 month in advance.** Never book ~1 week out.
- **Why:** prices rise every day as departure nears. Verified 2026-06-12: booking ~1 week out, ZIPAIR's cheap seats were **already sold out** and the only seats left were **¥1,158/leg (≈¥252k round trip)**. The same route booked 2–3 months ahead is ~¥69–86k/leg. Late booking = the single biggest money leak.

## 3. Flight shape (back-to-back, 1 day off)

- **Leave Tokyo Friday evening** (after work) → nonstop → **arrive SF Friday afternoon** (−16h timezone).
- **Full Friday night + all Saturday + Sunday in SF.**
- **Return Sunday from SFO → arrive Tokyo Monday afternoon** (westbound always +1 calendar day).
- **= 1 day off (Monday only).** Justifiable for a once-a-month routine. Back to work Tuesday.
