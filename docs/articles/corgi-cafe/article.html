A 24/7 cafe in a financial-district alley

Downtown, on 9 Claude Lane between Kearny and Grant, there is a place glowing past midnight. Corgi Cafe. The dog on the signage is in blue neon; the storefront reads OPEN 24/7.

A grand-opening banner hangs over the entrance. Walk in and the first thing you see is orange chairs, and people in hoodies typing on laptops.

Run by an AI insurance startup

Through the window the wall neon reads The First AI Insurance Company. There is the corgi logo, and the word "corgi" in 3D letters.

The cafe is run by Corgi, an AI insurance startup. CEO is 26-year-old Nico; co-founder Emily. YC 2024 grads, $108M Series A at a $630M valuation, aiming at a $1T market cap.

Corgi employees work 7 days a week, and 4-5 of them sleep at the office (the building is zoned residential, so it is legal). CEO Nico jokes, "if there is a fire, you will find my burnt body inside."

The cafe is the retail space on the first floor of HQ. Nico says, "when we tried to lease the 5th-floor office, the ground-floor storefront came bundled." Investors said no, loud; Corgi opened in February 2026 anyway. The bet is a brand play in the AI race.

How to get on the WiFi

Hold your iPhone up to the QR tablet on the counter and scan your order receipt.

You will be redirected to a screen with your WiFi code.

A 4-letter WiFi code appears. Enter it as the password for the network called Corgi Patrons. Cute name.

  • Valid for: 180 hours (about 7.5 days)
  • Devices: 2 (laptop + phone)
One order buys 7.5 days × 2 devices of WiFi. Less a coffee fee, more a 1-day coworking pass.

Inside: orange chairs and a sea of MacBooks

Three menu boards hang above the counter: Featured Drinks, Corgi Drinks, Smoothies. In the middle, a QR poster reads Get Insured?

The pillar up front advertises Boardy, an AI agent for networking.

The chairs are the same orange as the corgi mascot. About 70% of the customers are founders, MacBooks open. People here are talking VC, talking how to get into Founders Fund. The Silicon Valley sound.
The next Google could come out of this room.

I drank a cortado, $6.43. Y Combinator alums get 20% off.

The hiring flyer on each desk was the most SF thing about this place

The most San-Francisco thing in this cafe was not the coffee. It was the hiring flyer sitting on every table.

  • Role: Founder's Associate
  • Pay: $100k + 2-5% equity
  • Referral bonus: $5,000
  • They want: founders who bootstrapped to $2M in revenue, want to work 1-on-1 / can come into the office every day / can take hard feedback / live in the SF Bay Area
  • They do not want: people who wait for instructions / want 9-5 / fear failure

Who would (and would not) like it here

  • Would: AI founders who want to work in San Francisco at midnight / anyone who wants to breathe in startup smell
  • Would not: anyone coming to meet the corgi (the company dog Trudy only shows up sometimes)
Other cities are coming, supposedly: Salt Lake City, Atlanta, Chicago, Dallas, New York, London.
