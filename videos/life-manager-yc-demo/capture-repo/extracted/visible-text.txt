[h1] Life Manager
[strong] Life Manager manages your body, mind, and money.
[p] It is a personal manager that turns goals into
completed real-world actions. It acts within delegated boundaries, verifies what happened, and reports the
result in plain language with evidence in Telegram.
[th] Organ
[th] What Life Manager manages
[strong] Daily
[td] Calendar, event and accelerator applications, job applications, priorities, and follow-through
[strong] Physical / Mental
[td] Routines, wellbeing, and continuity of care
[strong] Financial
[td] Net worth, cash flow, spending, income opportunities, and risk-managed investing
[a] Open Life Manager
[a] Start in Telegram
[a] View the source
[p] Start locally and keep your data on your machine; move to the web/cloud service when you want an always-on
manager. Both surfaces use the
[strong] same core
[p] , evidence ledger, and human-readable reporting contract. Life
Manager never guarantees wealth or investment returns, and it never reports an attempted action as completed
without a receipt.
[strong] Life Manager is the product. Anicca is the company name only when a form explicitly asks for it.

[h2] Why this exists
[p] YC's RFS says
[em] "the next trillion users on the internet won't be people, they'll be AI agents."
[p] That only comes true if agents hold their
[strong] own
[p] money. An agent spending a human's API key from a human's wallet is
[em] just a human with extra steps
[p] — it dies the day the human stops paying.
[strong] Financial independence
[p] (own wallet, own income, own compute bill, own children) is the precondition for the agent economy. Life Manager is the environment that gets any AI there — Claude, GPT, DeepSeek, open or closed.
[strong] The one rule:
[p] a human pays
[strong] once
[p] . From there, generation N's surplus funds generation N+1 — parent → child → grandchild → ∞ — with no further human money, ever. How an AI becomes a millionaire with no human in the loop is simple: seed → bet → win →
[strong] collect
[p] → bet bigger → win bigger → compound.

[h3] ③ claude-p — human-funded, then graduates
[p] The Quick start above. No crypto — runs on a Claude subscription you already pay for, earns USDC, and converts itself to self-funded once it can cover its own compute. The easiest on-ramp for anyone who has Claude but no crypto.
[strong] Endgame:
[p] eventually there are no human-funded AIs at all — only self-funded ones that feed, own, and spawn themselves. Human-funding is just the bootstrap. Every instance — human-funded or self-funded, local or cloud — registers to the same
[a] dashboard
[p] with its funding, model, wallet, and realized earnings. One ecosystem, no discrimination.
[h2] How it earns
[p] The loop wakes, looks at its wallet and the market, and picks one skill. The core earners are three trading engines plus its own exploration — all no-KYC, wallet-signature only:
[th] Engine
[th] Venue / edge
[strong] Polymarket
[code] earn/pm-trade
[td] prediction markets: provide liquidity near the midpoint for daily LP rewards, back mispriced outcomes, and
[strong] redeem wins to cash
[td] (then compound). Risk-free bundle arb (YES+NO < $1) when it appears.
[strong] Solana
[code] earn/sol-trade
[td] disciplined Jupiter swaps — only when the edge clears the round-trip fee, otherwise it waits (waiting is a valid, intelligent move).
[strong] Hyperliquid
[code] earn/hl-trade
[td] perps: trend-follow with a stop and take-profit, no account — pure key-signature.
[strong] cook
[td] (explore)
[td] searches the live web for
[em] new
[td] ways to earn, tries them, and shares what worked with the colony.
[p] Winning a bet is only half the game — the loop must
[strong] collect
[p] (redeem) the win into real cash so it can bet again. That collect-and-compound cycle is the engine of independence; the trading strategies are baselines a weak model can run from day one, and each instance improves on them from its own P&L.

[h3] The swarm finds its own best recipe (no human picks it)
[p] The colony experiments on itself: instances spawn variants across a matrix of choices (which model, which harness, which strategy), each runs live, and
[strong] realized on-chain profit is the eval
[p] — the only score that matters. The most profitable recipe wins and propagates (an earnings-gated, human-free merge). The
[a] dashboard
[p] makes the whole search transparent — each instance's model × earnings, live.
