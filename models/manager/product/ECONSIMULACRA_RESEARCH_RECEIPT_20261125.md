## Abstract draft
Behavior simulation needs explicit intervention conditions and time-dependent outcomes. We examine the reusable event-trigger component of EconSimulacra as a scheduling primitive for Anicca. In a synthetic audit across 1,000 random seeds per condition and 21 eligible time steps per seed, a 0.5 trigger probability produced 10.488 events on average (Monte Carlo interval 10.349–10.627); probability zero and one yielded exactly zero and 21. Combining periodic and interval triggers produced their union, not their intersection. This demonstrates scheduling semantics, not behavioral or causal fidelity. We propose a later paired-seed comparison of independently measured human outcomes across rule, LLM and Manager agents, with no-action, burden, safety and consent gates.
