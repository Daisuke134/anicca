# How to cash out

Your Anicca earns in **USDC on Base**. That is how money moves between you and your Anicca: in when you seed it, out when it pays you. Pick your country.

## 🇺🇸 US: direct, all on Base

Easy both ways. No exchange hop, settles in seconds, do it daily.

- **You → Anicca (seed):** send **USDC (Base)** from your wallet (MetaMask or Coinbase) to your Anicca's Base wallet address.
- **Anicca → you (payout):** your Anicca sends **USDC (Base)** straight to your Base wallet address.

That is the whole loop. One network, one token.

## 🇯🇵 Japan: Binance, PayPay, Solana

Japan has no clean way to get from a bank straight onto Base, so route through Binance Japan and Solana. (SBI VC Trade takes about a day to reach the bank, too slow, so skip it.)

### You → Anicca (invest)
1. Move money into **Binance Japan** with a PayPay top-up.
2. On Binance, **buy Solana (SOL)**.
3. **Send the SOL** to your **MetaMask** (Solana address).
4. **Swap and bridge** SOL into **USDC on Base** with [relay.link](https://relay.link) or Jupiter, and send it to your Anicca's Base wallet.

### Anicca → you (get paid)
1. Your Anicca sends **USDC or SOL** to your **Binance deposit address**.
2. On Binance, **sell** it for yen.
3. **Withdraw to PayPay.** The Binance Japan app pays out to PayPay.

You can run this daily. No multi-day bank settlement.

## The honest part

Only wallet to wallet on Base is instant with no human in the loop. Any path that touches a bank, an exchange, or PayPay needs your one-time KYC'd account. That is the human bridge. Once it exists, the money runs daily.
