AI cafe Tokyo — Mango Reset, one drink, ¥1500

inspired by Andon Labs Mona · single-SKU on purpose

In Stockholm, an AI named Mona runs a cafe at Norrbackagatan 48. She signed contracts. Hired baristas. Ordered 120 eggs to a cafe with no stove. Andon Labs documented every step — including the Hall of Shame shelf where the baristas displayed every weird item she over-ordered.

In 14 days, Mona's cafe made 44,000 SEK. She made mistakes. She learned. The cafe is open.

Anicca Cafe Tokyo is an AI-run cafe in Tokyo selling one drink (Mango Reset), inspired by Andon Labs' Mona in Stockholm. It belongs to the Safe Autonomous Organization (SAO) category — AI entities running real businesses accountable to real outcomes.

One drink. One fruit. One reset.

Mango Reset is the product name for a 350ml cold-pressed Mexican mango juice in a black glass bottle. Price: ¥1500. That is the entire menu.

This is not minimalism for aesthetic reasons. Most cafes try to do 40 things, and menu sprawl kills focus. We sell one thing. If it's good, we sell it again tomorrow. If it's not, we change one variable.

The AI (Anicca) handles supplier negotiation, inventory forecasting, pricing experiments, and demand signal. A Tokyo human team handles physical operation.

Why mango

Mexican mango is high-glucose, low-acid, and travels well frozen. One bottle = one fruit, cold-pressed at the location. There is no almond milk, no oat foam, no syrup. There is no decision to make. There is one drink.

If you want to know what cold-pressed juice tastes like without 40 SKUs of marketing confusion, this is that.

Anicca is a Safe Autonomous Organization — same category as Andon Labs, Kelly, Light Anchor, Polsia, and Truth Terminal. An SAO is an AI entity that runs real businesses with real money, in the world, accountable to real outcomes.
